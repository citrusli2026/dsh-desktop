# dsh-desktop v0.2.2-rc.3.shell.0

DeepSeek Harness 社区桌面版 · 内置内核 `@deepseek-ai/dsh` 0.2.2-rc.3（由 0.2.2-alpha.3 升级，壳修订归零）。

## 本次更新

- **内核升级 0.2.2-alpha.3 → 0.2.2-rc.3**：32 个携带内核版本的 `dsh-*` pin 一起更新（manifest 共 38 个
  `dsh-*` 依赖），`@deepseek-ai/cordis` 跟随新内核到 4.1.0、`cordis-plugin-group` 到 `~1.1.0`；release-age
  豁免按 342 → 322 条重钉，闭包锁文件重生成后重新部署，peer 审计 `closure satisfied`。此前从 rc.1 起
  `dsh-watch` 连续十次（issue #142–#154）卡在「重建 Harness 闭包」这步：rc.2 起上游改为压缩发布 `client`
  包，旧的账号设置补丁再也匹配不到整行（`ERR_PNPM_PATCH_FAILED`）。
- **两处维护补丁改由部署期应用**：账号设置的两处修改（登录弹窗被浏览器拦截时的兜底跳转、`dshDesktop`
  能力探测同时确认 onboarding 桥）不再以 pnpm patch 形式存在——压缩后的单行 bundle 里 unified diff 既无法
  人工复核也无法整行匹配。它们改由 `scripts/patch-account-ui.mjs` 在部署与打包时应用：精确锚点 + 命中
  计数断言、幂等，`REVIEWED_KERNELS` 之外的内核直接让构建失败而不是静默出包。会话垃圾桶的生命周期补丁
  同样新增压缩版锚点分支，两处补丁的落地既有 `node --check` 也有行为单测覆盖。
- **桌面工具浮层高度减半**（社区反馈：展开后太长，要滚动才够得着最后一个按钮）：连接组两个入口保持整行，
  恢复／维护／信息三组改成双列网格（9 行变 6 行），飞书卡片收成「标题 + 一行三格状态 + 一排操作」。实测
  中文（浅色）886px → 492px、英文（深色）886px → 505px，约 44%；顶部说明、飞书提示与英文长标签改为最多
  两行，不再从中间截断。面板高度新增 e2e 门禁（≤540px）并断言双列结构，两种语言各一条。
- **依赖**：`electron` 44.9.3 → 44.10.2、`@playwright/test` 1.64.1 → 1.65.0（dependabot #151、#152）。

## English Summary

- **Kernel 0.2.2-alpha.3 → 0.2.2-rc.3**: the 32 `dsh-*` pins that carry the kernel version moved together (the
  manifest carries 38 `dsh-*` dependencies), cordis follows to 4.1.0 with `cordis-plugin-group ~1.1.0`,
  release-age exemptions were re-pinned (342 → 322), and the regenerated closure deploys with
  `audit-harness-peers: closure satisfied`. Attempts to land rc.1 through rc.3 had failed ten times
  (issues #142–#154) at "Rebuild the harness closure": since rc.2 upstream minifies the published client
  bundles, so the account patch no longer matched a whole line (`ERR_PNPM_PATCH_FAILED`).
- **Both maintenance patches are applied at deploy time**: the two account changes (redirect the pre-opened tab
  when the browser blocks the login popup; require the onboarding bridge in the `dshDesktop` capability probe)
  are no longer a pnpm patch — in a single-line minified bundle a unified diff is neither reviewable nor
  matchable. `scripts/patch-account-ui.mjs` applies them during deploy and packaging with exact anchors, hit
  counts, and idempotence, and refuses any kernel outside `REVIEWED_KERNELS` instead of shipping silently. The
  session-trash lifecycle patch gained the minified anchor branch, and both are covered by `node --check` plus
  behavioural unit tests.
- **The desktop-tools overlay is half as tall** (community feedback: too long to reach the last button without
  scrolling): the two connect entries stay full width while recovery, maintenance and information pair their
  actions in a two-column grid (nine rows become six), and the Feishu card folds into a heading, one three-cell
  status line, and its action chips. Measured 886px → 492px (Chinese, light) and 886px → 505px (English, dark),
  about 44% shorter; the panel copy, the Feishu hint and long English labels may now wrap to two lines instead
  of being cut mid-sentence. A new e2e gate pins the panel at 540px and asserts the grid, per locale.
- **Dependencies**: `electron` 44.9.3 → 44.10.2 and `@playwright/test` 1.64.1 → 1.65.0 (dependabot #151, #152).
